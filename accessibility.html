Accessibility

A website for more visitors

TRGVC aims to make its creator website usable with a keyboard, assistive technology and a range of screen sizes.

What is provided?

  • A skip-to-content link and semantic navigation.
  • Visible keyboard focus indicators on links, buttons and settings.
  • Labels for important interactive controls.
  • Support for reduced-motion system preferences.
  • Alternative links when visitors prefer not to load YouTube and Spotify embeds.

Known limitations

Third-party YouTube and Spotify players have accessibility controls outside our direct control. Caption availability varies by video. Some parts of the visual design may still need independent contrast and screen-reader testing.

Report a problem

If you find an accessibility problem, contact info@trgvc.club, ideally with the affected page, device and a description of the issue. We will review reasonable reports and improve the experience when possible.

Statement prepared: 15 November 2026. This is not a claim of audited WCAG conformance.
