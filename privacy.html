Privacy Policy

How TRGVC handles information

TRGVC is a UK-based, independent personal music and gaming creator website. This notice describes information processed when you visit trgvc.club and choose to use external media.

Contact: The individual operating this site publishes under the creator name TRGVC. For privacy enquiries, including information about the operator, email info@trgvc.club. Whether further identity or address details must be published depends on the operator's legal circumstances and should be reviewed before launch.

1. Who is responsible?

TRGVC is the creator identity used by the individual responsible for this website. You can send data-protection questions or requests to info@trgvc.club. This site does not describe itself as a registered limited company.

2. What information may be handled?

  • Technical visit data: our hosting and security provider, Cloudflare, may process IP addresses, timestamps, browser details and requests for delivery, troubleshooting and protection.
  • Emails you send: your email address, message and other details you choose to include are handled to answer your enquiry.
  • Your media settings: the site remembers whether you chose to enable YouTube and Spotify in first-party browser localStorage. The site does not transmit these settings to us.
  • Optional YouTube and Spotify integrations: after you enable them, your browser can connect directly to those services, which process information under their own privacy policies.

The supplied website code does not include an account system, email newsletter sign-up, advertising pixel or first-party visitor analytics. This description must be updated if these services are added.

3. Why is information used?

Hosting/security requests are handled to make the website available and protect it. Where UK GDPR applies, legitimate interests in operating and securing a public creator website may be a relevant lawful basis, subject to balancing visitors' interests. Email enquiries are processed to respond to requests; the lawful basis depends on the circumstances of each message. Optional embeds and any non-essential storage technologies are disabled until chosen by the visitor.

4. YouTube API Services

TRGVC uses YouTube API Services on the server to retrieve public playlist video information, such as titles, video IDs and publication dates, when a visitor enables YouTube features. We do not ask visitors to sign into a Google account or collect their YouTube account information. YouTube embedded players and thumbnail requests are loaded only after the visitor chooses to enable YouTube. Use of YouTube features is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Spotify media

Album cover images are served from TRGVC’s own website. Spotify player frames are disabled until you enable Spotify. When enabled, the player connects to Spotify or its content delivery network. See Spotify's Privacy Policy. Spotify may independently set or access storage technologies or otherwise process data.

6. Providers and international handling

Cloudflare provides hosting and security services. Optional external providers include YouTube/Google and Spotify. Their systems may process information outside the UK. Their own privacy information explains relevant arrangements. We do not sell your contact details from email enquiries.

7. How long is information kept?

TRGVC keeps email enquiries only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, manage an ongoing matter or meet applicable legal requirements, then reviews them for deletion. Cloudflare determines some technical log retention under its services and settings; exact periods must be checked against the operator's Cloudflare configuration. You can clear the site's saved media preferences using browser storage controls.

8. Your rights

Depending on circumstances and applicable law, you may have rights to access, rectify or erase information, restrict or object to processing, and complain to the UK's Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). To make a request, contact info@trgvc.club. We may need enough information to verify a request without collecting unnecessary identification data.

9. External sites and changes

Links to YouTube, Spotify, Steam and other platforms lead to independent websites with their own policies. This notice will be updated when website services or data practices change. Last reviewed: 15 November 2026.
