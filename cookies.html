Cookies & Media Choices

Control optional third-party content

TRGVC is designed to show its basic content without loading YouTube videos or Spotify players until you choose to enable them.

1. First-party preferences

Your browser may store trgvc-privacy-choices-v1 in localStorage. It records two choices, youtube and spotify. This is used only to remember and honour your selection; the site does not send the values to the server.

2. YouTube

When YouTube is off, the site shows local placeholders and normal links to YouTube. When you enable it, playlist data is retrieved through our same-origin Cloudflare Worker, and your browser may connect to YouTube's privacy-enhanced player and thumbnail service. Google may use its own cookies or other technologies. See Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Spotify

Album artwork and labels are served from the TRGVC website regardless of your Spotify preference. When you enable Spotify, its embedded player can load; Spotify may use cookies or other storage technologies. See Spotify's Privacy Policy.

4. Cloudflare

Cloudflare may apply security-related cookies or other necessary technologies when it protects or delivers this website. The exact technologies depend on the site's active Cloudflare features and should be audited by the site operator.

5. Changing your preferences

Use Privacy settings in the footer of the homepage at any time. You can accept both categories, accept one, or reject optional services. Disabling a service removes its player from the page; it cannot erase data already processed by a third party when that service was previously enabled.

6. Your browser

Browser settings also let you manage cookies and local storage. Deleting site storage resets TRGVC preferences, so you will be asked again on your next visit.

Last reviewed: 15 November 2026. The actual site should be checked with browser developer tools and the operator's Cloudflare dashboard before claiming full compliance.
