Ownership, gameplay and music

TRGVC combines original creative work with commentary and gameplay involving third-party games and platforms.

Original TRGVC music, graphic branding, written material and videos may be protected by copyright. A copyright notice does not replace any underlying licence or proof of ownership. Unless the rights holder gives permission or an exception applies, please do not copy, reupload or commercially redistribute protected original material.

2. Third-party game content

Video game names, trademarks, artwork, gameplay assets and game audio are owned by the relevant publishers and creators. Gameplay usage remains subject to applicable publisher terms, licences and copyright exceptions. TRGVC is not officially affiliated with these publishers solely because their games appear on this website.

3. Music rights and permissions

Only publish or distribute tracks, samples, vocals, visual assets and recordings for which the creator has appropriate rights or permission. Any distribution or collection arrangement may involve additional third-party rights, so this page does not assert ownership of everything embedded here.

4. Reporting a rights concern

For a copyright or attribution concern, email info@trgvc.club with the work, URL, rights claimed and a way to contact you. Requests will be reviewed and, where appropriate, the affected material corrected or removed.
